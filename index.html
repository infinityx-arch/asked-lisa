There’s something I’ve been wanting to tell you, and I think there’s no better way to say it than from my heart.

I don’t know exactly what the future will look like, and I know that life won’t always be easy. There will be days when everything feels right, when we laugh until we forget the time, and when simply being together makes the whole world feel a little brighter.

But I also know there may be days when things get difficult. Days when we misunderstand each other, when we’re tired, when we make mistakes, or when life doesn’t go the way we hoped.

And if there’s one thing I want you to know, it’s that I don’t want to be here only for the easy days.

I want to be here for the hard ones, too.

If I could choose one place to be right now,
it would be anywhere I could share a moment with you.

I want us to learn how to understand each other, to listen when one of us needs to be heard, to apologize when we’re wrong, and to keep choosing kindness even when things aren’t perfect.

I don’t expect either of us to be perfect. We both have our flaws, our insecurities, our weaknesses, and the parts of ourselves we’re still learning to understand.

I want us to accept each other as we are, while also helping each other grow into better versions of ourselves.

I want to celebrate your happiness, support your dreams, comfort you when you’re sad, and remind you of your worth on the days when you forget it.

I want the little things with you. The random conversations, the silly jokes, the quiet moments, the unexpected adventures, and even the ordinary days that somehow become special just because you’re there.

I’m not promising that I’ll always know the right words or that I’ll never make mistakes. But I want to keep learning, keep communicating, and keep making an honest effort to treat your heart with care.

Even when life isn’t perfect,
I hope we can still find a little light in each other.

Because to me, love isn’t about finding someone who has no flaws. It’s about finding someone whose imperfections you understand, whose growth you want to support, and whose hand you want to hold as you both navigate this unpredictable life.

And if you let me, I’d love to discover that kind of love with you.

Not just when the sun is shining, but when the skies turn gray, too.

Not just when life is easy, but when we have to face its challenges together.

Because I don’t want something that only looks beautiful in the perfect moments.

I want something real, something warm, something honest, and something we both feel safe choosing, day after day.

And right now, the person I want to ask to begin that journey with me is you.

Wherever this road goes,
I’d like to walk it right next to you.
